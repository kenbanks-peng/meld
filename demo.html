01 / The big picture

Executive summary

On track

Delivery accelerated without trading away reliability. Smaller changes, a shorter CI pipeline, and clearer service ownership helped the team ship 226 production deployments this quarter. The next focus is reducing background-job latency as usage grows.

The quarter’s biggest win

Build caching cut median pipeline time from 14 minutes to 8 minutes, giving engineers more time for the work that matters.
